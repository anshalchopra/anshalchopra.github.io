Work

What I do.

Some problems need better information. Others need a redesigned process, an automation, an AI capability that actually earns its place, a cleaner cloud setup, or a systems integration that finally makes two teams speak the same language. I look at the whole picture before deciding what to build.

Dashboards & decision support

Help people see what matters.

I start with the decision, not the chart. I work out who needs the information, what they need to understand, and which measures will genuinely help. The result should make the next conversation easier, not add another report to maintain.

The question
What does someone need to understand or decide?
Typical output
KPI framework, dashboard, or reporting view

Process & automation

Make everyday work simpler.

Before automating anything, I look at how the work actually happens. I map the steps, handoffs, delays, and exceptions, then identify what can be removed, redesigned, or automated. AI can be part of that answer when it solves a real need, not simply because it is available.

The question
Where does work slow down, repeat, or lose context?
Typical output
Process maps, requirements, or an automation brief

Analysis & forecasting

Turn uncertainty into a clearer view forward.

I break broad questions into something we can examine, test, and explain. That can include finding the cause of a change, building a forecast, or comparing possible scenarios. I am careful about assumptions because a useful answer should also be an honest one.

The question
What changed, why, and what could happen next?
Typical output
Root-cause analysis, forecast, or scenario view

Systems, cloud & integrations

Help tools and teams work together.

Business needs can get lost between people, platforms, and technical decisions. I translate those needs into clear requirements, data flows, integrations, and documentation so the solution makes sense to the people building it and the people using it.

The question
How should people, data, and platforms connect?
Typical output
Requirements, context diagram, data flow, or integration notes
